Taxonomy

Define your primary and secondary navigation structure. First-level items appear as main nav sections and can have an icon. Second-level items are pages or sub-sections within them.

Navigation pattern
Persistent sidebar
Full-width sidebar always visible with labels. Standard for most enterprise apps.
Collapsible sidebar
Collapses to icon-only rail to save space. Expands on hover or click.
Top nav tabs
Horizontal tabs directly below the header. Clean for apps with 3 to 6 top-level sections.
Dual-level nav
Top bar owns global section switching. Sidebar owns in-section pages. Scales to complex apps.
Hamburger + drawer
Nav hidden by default, opens as an overlay. Maximizes content space. Common on mobile-first apps.
Command palette
Cmd+K launcher as primary navigation. Best for power users with predictable workflows.
