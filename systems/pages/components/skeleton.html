Skeleton

Shimmer placeholders that mirror the shape of loading content. Reduces perceived wait time by showing structure before data arrives.

Skeleton variants
Rect variant="rect" · Default. Rectangular placeholder for images, cards, banners, and arbitrary blocks.
Circle variant="circle" · Circular placeholder for avatars, icons, and profile images.
Text variant="text" · Multi-line text block. The last line is 65% width to simulate natural line breaks.
Composed mixed variants · Combine circle, rect, and text variants to match the shape of your loading content.

General fresh-skeleton
  • •Mirror the shape of the content being loaded as closely as possible. Use variant="circle" for avatars, variant="text" for paragraphs, and variant="rect" for images and blocks.
  • •Replace the entire skeleton immediately when data arrives. Do not show a partial mix of skeleton and loaded content in the same logical section.
  • •Use skeleton screens for content that loads in under 5 seconds. For longer waits, add a progress bar or spinner with an informational label explaining the operation.
  • •Set width and height to match the actual rendered dimensions of the element they represent. Skeletons that are visibly smaller or larger than the content cause jarring layout shifts on load.
  • •Do not show skeleton screens for operations triggered by user actions (button clicks). Use a loading spinner on the button or inline spinner instead, which communicates that the system is responding.

Accessibility

Skeleton satisfies all universal accessibility concerns. Component-specific concerns are below.

ConcernRequirement
screen readerSkeleton bones are decorative. Wrap the skeleton group in a container with aria-busy="true" and aria-label describing what is loading. Remove aria-busy when content is ready.
animationThe shimmer animation respects prefers-reduced-motion if your target environment sets it. Consider pausing animation for users who have requested reduced motion via a CSS media query.
