General fresh-drawer
  • •Use fresh-drawer for contextual tasks that benefit from appearing alongside the main content: settings panels, filter drawers, detail views, and side-by-side editing.
  • •Default to placement="right" for most use cases. Use placement="left" only when the drawer supplements left-side navigation or filter panels that pair with left-rail layouts.
  • •Open the drawer with element.show() and close it with element.close(). Clicking the backdrop or pressing Escape will also close it automatically.
  • •Always include a title and a footer with at least a cancel action. Never leave the user without a clear way to dismiss the drawer.
  • •Prefer a modal over a drawer when the user must make a decision before doing anything else. Use a drawer when the task is supplementary and the user may want to reference the page behind it.

Accessibility

Drawer satisfies all universal accessibility concerns. Component-specific concerns are below.

ConcernRequirement
Role and labellingThe panel has role="dialog" and aria-modal="true". When a title is set, aria-labelledby connects it to the heading element.
Escape to closePressing Escape closes the drawer. Clicking the backdrop also closes it. Both are required keyboard and pointer interactions.
Scroll lockSets document.body.style.overflow = 'hidden' when open. Restored on close. Ensure content within the drawer body scrolls independently for long forms.
