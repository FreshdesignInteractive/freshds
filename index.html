What it is

Three tools. One workflow.

Freshdesign gives product managers and designers everything needed to go from brand brief to working prototype — without a front-end engineer in the room.

Design system
60+ components built as standard web components. Token-driven, framework-agnostic, dark and light mode out of the box.
Pattern library
Hundreds of pre-built screens across every major category — auth, onboarding, dashboards, AI & chat, settings, and more.
Configurator
Point, click, set your brand colors and fonts. Export a token file your AI coding tool can use immediately.
How it works

Four steps from brief to prototype

01
Set your brand
Open the configurator. Drop in your primary color, pick a font. Done in under a minute.
02
Browse patterns
Find the screens you need from hundreds of ready-made layouts. Filter by category.
03
Export tokens
Download your brand tokens as a single file. Every component inherits them automatically.
04
Build with AI
Hand the tokens to Claude Code or Cursor. Get brand-correct screens in seconds, not hours.
