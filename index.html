How it works

Four steps to AI-ready design

01
Customize your design system
Set your brand colors, typography, and visual style using the theme designer. Everything else is already built and ready to go.
02
Fine-tune your rules and constraints
FreshDS comes with industry-wide UX best practices already baked in. Review and adjust them to match your company's specific guidelines.
03
Download your design system
Export your configured DS as a complete, self-contained package. Ready to deploy on any machine, any environment.
04
Build with your AI tool of choice
Point any AI coding tool at your downloaded DS. Your brand and UX guidelines are already built in, so every output is on-brand from the start.
