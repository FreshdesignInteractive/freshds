Objective 1: Grow ARR to $8M

Drive new business acquisition across Enterprise and Mid-Market segments. Focus on strategic accounts with multi-year deal potential and expansion revenue from existing customers upgrading to higher-tier plans.

Key Results: Close 12 Enterprise deals ≥ $50K · Achieve NRR of 115% · Reduce sales cycle to < 45 days

Objective 2: Improve Win Rate to 35%

Strengthen competitive positioning through better discovery, proof-of-concept delivery, and executive alignment. The competitive landscape has shifted — we need to lead with AI differentiation in every deal.

Objective 3: Build Pipeline Coverage 3×

Each AE should maintain a pipeline of at least 3× quota to absorb natural churn and deal slippage. SDR headcount growing from 4 to 7 in Q2 will accelerate top-of-funnel volume targeting ICP accounts in SaaS and FinTech verticals.

Pipeline review every Friday. Deal health scoring now automated via the lead enrichment pipeline — focus your review time on stage 3+ deals and at-risk accounts flagged by churn prediction.
