Jun 2, 2026
I want to redesign our onboarding flow. Currently users drop off at step 3 (profile setup). What are the common reasons for this? Step 3 drop-off is very common for a few reasons: form fatigue (too many required fields), perceived low value (users don't understand why the info is needed), and friction at avatar upload (users don't have a photo ready). Research suggests asking for the minimum viable info and letting users complete their profile later. We require first name, last name, job title, timezone, and a profile photo. What's the minimum we actually need? For onboarding, you likely need only first name (for personalisation) and timezone (for scheduling features). Job title helps with personalisation but can wait. Profile photo almost never needs to be required — make it optional with a generated avatar fallback. Last name can be deferred until billing.
Jun 3, 2026
We reduced the form to just first name and timezone. Drop-off improved from 42% to 18%. Now step 4 (workspace setup) is the new problem. Any ideas? Great result! For workspace setup drop-off, the URL slug field is typically the friction point — users don't understand what it's for or feel anxious about "getting it wrong." Try:

1. Auto-generate the slug from the workspace name with a live preview
2. Make it editable but not prominent — collapse it behind "Advanced options"
3. Remove industry/team size from this step — collect it in-product after activation when users are more invested Can you draft the revised step copy for workspace setup? Here's revised copy for the workspace setup step:

Heading: "Name your workspace"
Subheading: "This is where your team will work. You can change it anytime."

Field label: Workspace name (remove "URL" field from primary view)
Placeholder: e.g. "Acme Design" or "Marketing Team"

CTA: "Continue" (not "Next" — feels more purposeful)
Skip link: Remove entirely — removing skip links at critical steps increases completion by ~15% in Appcues data.
