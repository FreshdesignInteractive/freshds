Responsive variants

Prefix a span with a breakpoint to override it at that viewport width. Variants are additive, base class applies below the breakpoint.

ClassApplies atExample use
.fds-col-{n}All sizesDefault (mobile-first) span
.fds-col-sm-{n}≥ 480pxLarge phone layout adjustment
.fds-col-md-{n}≥ 768pxTablet layout: 1→2 columns
.fds-col-lg-{n}≥ 1024pxDesktop layout: 2→3 columns
