Containers

Media card

Full-bleed thumbnail card with a title and optional description footer. Use for pattern libraries, galleries, and any grid of visual items.

Examples
Attributes
AttributeValuesDescription
titlestringCard label (required)
descriptionstringOptional secondary line below title
srcstringImage URL — renders <img> in thumb area
hrefstringMakes the card a link
targetstringLink target, e.g. _blank
ratiostringThumbnail aspect ratio (default: 16/10)
Slots
SlotDescription
thumbCustom thumbnail content — fills the top area at full bleed
