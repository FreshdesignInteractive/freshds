Confidence Badge

Displays AI model certainty as a percentage with semantic color. Green for high confidence (80+), amber for medium (50-79), red for low (below 50). Optional progress bar adds a visual fill layer.

Confidence badge states
High confidence value="94" · 80 and above. Green pill signals reliable output.
Medium confidence value="67" · 50 to 79. Amber signals caution, user should verify.
Low confidence value="31" · Below 50. Red signals the model is uncertain. Prompt user review.
With progress bar show-bar · Expanded format with a mini bar and visible label. Good for comparison panels.
Custom label label="Match score" · Label adapts to domain context: Match score, Accuracy, Certainty.

General fresh-confidence-badge
  • •Only show confidence badges when the model returns a meaningful probability score. Do not fabricate or estimate scores client-side.
  • •When confidence is below 50, pair the badge with a tooltip or inline note explaining what the low score means and what the user should do next.
  • •Use show-bar when displaying multiple scores side-by-side in a comparison panel. The bar makes relative differences easier to scan.
  • •Adapt the label attribute to your domain context: "Match score" for search, "Certainty" for classification, "Confidence" for general QA.
  • •Never block user actions based solely on a low confidence score. Show the badge as informational context, then let the user decide.
