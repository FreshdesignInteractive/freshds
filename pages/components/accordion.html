General fresh-accordion
  • •Use fresh-accordion for progressive disclosure of related content groups. Do not use it for navigation: use fresh-sidebar or fresh-topbar-menu instead.
  • •Default to single-open mode (no multiple). Only add multiple when users genuinely need to compare content across items simultaneously.
  • •Use the open attribute to pre-expand the most contextually relevant item. Do not pre-expand more than one item in single-open mode.
  • •Keep label text short and scannable: under 60 characters. The label is the entire affordance for collapsed content, so clarity is critical.
  • •Use disabled with a label that explains why the item is unavailable (e.g. "Requires upgrade"). Never leave users wondering why they cannot interact.

<fresh-accordion>
  <fresh-accordion-item label="What is FreshDS?" open>
    FreshDS is a token-driven design system for AI-native enterprise apps.
  </fresh-accordion-item>
  <fresh-accordion-item label="How do I create a client theme?">
    Open the Theme and export your CSS tokens.
  </fresh-accordion-item>
  <fresh-accordion-item label="Restricted (requires upgrade)" disabled>
    Content not available.
  </fresh-accordion-item>
</fresh-accordion>
