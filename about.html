About Freshdesign

Built for the AI era of design

FreshDS started from a simple observation: AI coding tools are fast, but they still start from scratch every time. We're changing that.

Our mission

Give every team a production-ready head start

Design systems have always been the secret weapon of great product teams. But they take months to build and years to maintain — a luxury only large engineering orgs could afford.

Freshdesign changes the equation. Set your brand colors once. Get a complete, token-driven design system that every AI coding tool can use out of the box. No more blank canvas. No more reinventing buttons.

We believe the next generation of products will be built by small, focused teams moving fast with AI. FreshDS is the foundation they need to move fast without sacrificing quality.

  • 2024

What we believe

Principles behind every decision

Tokens over hardcodes
Every color, size, and spacing value is a token. Brand changes propagate everywhere — no manual find-and-replace, no drift.
Framework-agnostic
Standard web components work with React, Vue, Svelte, plain HTML — or whatever your AI coding tool generates. No lock-in.
Patterns, not just components
Components are building blocks. What teams need are complete, real-world patterns — auth flows, dashboards, onboarding, AI interfaces.
The maker

Built by a designer who codes

Freshdesign is a solo product. That means every decision — from the token architecture to the pattern library to this website — is made by one person who cares deeply about quality.

No committee. No lowest-common-denominator decisions. Just a strong point of view on what good design systems look like, refined over years of doing this work with real teams.
