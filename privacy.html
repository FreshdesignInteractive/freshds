Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026  ·  Freshdesign, operated by Rajesh Sidharthan, California, USA

Short version: We collect only what we need to run the beta — your name and email from Google or GitHub sign-in. We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

Freshdesign ("we", "us", "our") is a design system product operated by Rajesh Sidharthan, based in California, USA. You can reach us at raj.sidharthan@freshdesign.com.

2. What we collect and why

When you sign in to the beta using Google or GitHub OAuth, we receive and store:

  • Email address — to identify your account and communicate with you about the beta
  • Full name — displayed in your profile
  • Profile photo URL — displayed as your avatar (we link to it; we don't host it)
  • OAuth provider — to know whether you signed in via Google or GitHub

We also collect feedback you voluntarily submit through the in-product feedback tool, including the message text and the page you were on when you sent it.

We do not collect passwords. We do not collect payment information during the beta. We do not use tracking pixels or behavioral ad targeting.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use Supabase for authentication. Supabase stores a session token in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in between visits. This token expires automatically and contains no personally identifiable information beyond what's needed to verify your session. We set no advertising or analytics cookies.

4. How we use your information

  • To authenticate you and maintain your beta session
  • To enforce the 100-seat beta limit (we count active profiles)
  • To read and respond to feedback you submit in the product
  • To contact you about beta updates, new features, or when the product launches publicly

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Who we share data with

We use the following sub-processors to operate the service:

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting (data stored in the US). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Vercel — web hosting and content delivery (data stored in the US). Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Google / GitHub — OAuth identity providers. We receive only the profile data you authorize at sign-in.

We do not share your data with any other third parties.

6. Data retention

We retain your profile data for as long as you have an active account. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your profile record from our database within 30 days. Some anonymized, aggregate data (such as total seat counts) may be retained for internal analytics.

7. California privacy rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know — you may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to delete — you may request that we delete your personal information
  • Right to opt out of sale — we do not sell personal information, so there is nothing to opt out of
  • Right to non-discrimination — exercising any of these rights will not affect your access to the product

To exercise any of these rights, email raj.sidharthan@freshdesign.com with the subject line "CCPA Request". We will respond within 45 days.

8. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data: HTTPS for all traffic, Supabase row-level security (RLS) policies so users can only access their own records, and OAuth-only authentication (no passwords stored). No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Freshdesign is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If we make material changes, we will notify beta users by email. Continued use of the product after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at raj.sidharthan@freshdesign.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
